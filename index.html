Project 01 · JPMorgan Chase · Leadership Edge

EXPLORE: For Aspiring Managers

My role
Sole designer and developer, end to end
Audience
Employees who aspire to become people managers
Format
3-part interactive PDF learning journey
Tools
Adobe InDesign, Adobe Acrobat, internal LLM

The Problem:

Employees who wanted to move into management needed a realistic picture of what it means to be a manager at JPMorgan Chase before they made the leap. They also needed a way to start building management skills and their network, with their own manager involved in the conversation. Leadership Edge serves roughly 40,000 managers across new managers, experienced managers and executives, and EXPLORE gave the pipeline before that first role its own starting point.

My Process:

  1. DiscoveryRan a needs analysis with the Leadership Edge team to define the audience, the goals of the journey and what success would look like.
  2. Designed the journeyStoryboarded a three-part interactive journey that moves learners from understanding the role, to building skills, to planning next steps.
  3. Built and testedBuilt all branching and interactivity myself. Used our internal LLM to speed up first-draft copy for manager-capability content and to create variations of reflection prompts for user testing.
  4. LaunchedManaged the project timeline and published the finished journey to the Leadership Edge LMS.

The Solution:

EXPLORE is a self-paced, fully interactive journey that gives aspiring managers insight into the role, resources to build management skills, and a plan for growing their network.

  • Manager partnership built in: Learners schedule a conversation with their manager, using guided discussion topics and a plan for regular follow-up meetings.
  • Reflection at every step: Built-in notes and reflection prompts, refined through user testing, turn reading into personal planning.
  • Beyond the course: A "Beyond EXPLORE" section and curated resources help learners act on a development plan, seek feedback and build their network.
  • Clear expectations: The journey is honest about what it is: a way to explore the role, not a guarantee of a manager position.
  • Reach: Together with Generational Leadership, EXPLORE reached more than 1,000 learners.

Project 02 · JPMorgan Chase · Leadership Edge

Generational Leadership

My role
Designer and developer, including narration and accessibility
Audience
Managers of early career talent
Format
Audio-narrated eLearning with closed captions
Tools
Articulate Storyline

The Problem:

One of the most frequent requests Leadership Edge received from managers was help leading Gen Z employees. Today's teams include four generations, and managers bringing on new early career joiners needed practical ways to motivate, inspire and engage them, grounded in real workforce data rather than stereotypes.

My Process:

  1. ResearchedGathered generational workforce data and sources to build an accurate picture of today's multigenerational workplace.
  2. Focused on behaviorsStructured the content around practical things managers can do, so every section ends in an action, not just a fact.
  3. Planned narrationWrote and planned audio narration to make the course easy to follow and more personal.
  4. Built for accessDeveloped the course with closed captioning and clear, consistent navigation so every manager could use it.

The Solution:

A narrated, caption-ready eLearning course that gives managers an overview of the four generations in the workforce and best practices for leading early career talent.

  • Research-grounded: Content is built on cited workforce research, giving managers context they can trust.
  • Behavior-focused: Best practices are framed as concrete manager actions for motivating and engaging new joiners.
  • Accessible by design: Audio narration, closed captions and play controls on every slide support different learning needs.
  • High demand, strong feedback: Built to answer a top manager request, the course consistently earned positive feedback for opening up new insights and giving managers ideas for leading multigenerational teams.
  • Reach: Together with EXPLORE, reached more than 1,000 learners.

Project 04 · CIBC US

IRA Fundamentals & Error Prevention

My role
Learning experience designer and developer
Audience
Branch and client support teams in private, personal and digital banking
Format
Self-paced eLearning: 3 lessons and a final quiz
Tools
Articulate Rise 360

The Problem:

IRAs are something most branch and client support team members don't handle often. So when a client walks in or calls about one, transactions and processes are frequently done incorrectly. Each error means rework, delays for the client and compliance risk. The business requested a course aimed squarely at the mistakes we see most.

My Process:

  1. Partnered with SMEs and leadershipWorked with subject matter experts to pinpoint the most common IRA errors and where they happen, and with leadership to align on goals.
  2. Outlined and got approvalStarted with a course outline and took it through an approval review with SMEs and leadership before any design work began.
  3. Storyboarded and iteratedWrote a full storyboard and refined it through several rounds of SME and leadership review, tightening accuracy and scenarios each time.
  4. Developed the courseBuilt the approved storyboard in Articulate Rise 360, with practice and knowledge checks throughout.

The Solution:

A three-lesson course that builds from the basics to the errors themselves: IRA foundations, concepts, types and rules; matching IRA solutions to client profiles; and preventing and resolving common IRA transaction errors, followed by a final quiz.

  • Designed around real errors: Content is organized by the error categories teams actually run into: establishment, contribution and distribution.
  • Scenario-based practice: Client-profile flashcards and beneficiary case studies, such as an equal split among children and spouse versus non-spouse beneficiaries, show the rules in real situations.
  • Know when to escalate: Each scenario set ends with clear guidance on when to bring in specialized support.
  • Check as you go: Knowledge checks reinforce key decisions, like Traditional versus Roth, before learners move on.
  • Approval built in: Outline and storyboard reviews with SMEs and leadership kept the content accurate before development.
  • Result: 30% fewer IRA errors within 4 weeks of launch, meaning less rework and more confidence handling IRA transactions.

Project 05 · CIBC US

SharePoint Video Series

My role
Sole video producer: scripted, recorded, edited and published
Audience
Client Support Center team members
Format
3-part microlearning series, screen-capture video
Tools
Camtasia, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, internal LLM, Copilot

The Problem:

The Client Support Center was launching a brand-new SharePoint hub: one home for the team site, news, center updates, job aids and procedures. The team needed to find, organize and save files in the new space right away, without slowing down the client support work they do every day. A long training session or a dense written guide would have pulled people away from the phones and been forgotten by the time they needed it.

My Process:

  1. Listened to the teamMet with Client Support Center managers and SMEs to learn how they actually used their files and where they expected people to get stuck.
  2. Chunked the contentBroke the launch into the three tasks people needed first, so each video answers one question in a few minutes.
  3. Storyboarded and scripted with AIUsed our internal LLM to generate first-draft scripts and storyboards from the objectives and SME notes, then edited them for accuracy, tone and pacing and reviewed them with SMEs and managers before recording.
  4. Built, reviewed and launchedScreen-captured in Camtasia, added motion graphics in After Effects, edited in Premiere Pro, ran an SME review, and published the series to the new site.

The Solution:

A three-part microlearning series that walks learners through the new site in the order they will use it: getting oriented, creating a shortcut to the team's files, and saving files to the right place.

  • Microlearning design: Each video covers one task, keeping cognitive load low and making it easy to rewatch the exact step someone needs.
  • Built with SMEs: Scripts were validated by Client Support Center SMEs and managers, so every click shown matches the real site.
  • Performance support: Designed to be used at the moment of need, not just watched once at launch.
  • Scalable: The approach became the model for SharePoint training across the wider organization, including SharePoint 101 and site owner training.
  • Well received: The series earned positive feedback from Client Support Center managers and team members.

Project 06 · CIBC US

Project Lightyear Demos

My role
Learning experience designer
Audience
Branch bankers, client support, digital banking and private banking teams
Format
Series of video demos of the new mobile app and online banking site
Tools
Camtasia, Adobe Premiere Pro

The Problem:

Project Lightyear replaced the bank's digital banking experience with a new mobile app and online banking website. Before launch, the people supporting clients needed to know the new platform well enough to answer questions and guide clients through it, often before they had used it themselves. The timeline was tight, the audience was large and varied, and the platform was still changing: every demo had to be captured in a test environment while screens were being updated.

My Process:

  1. Partnered with the project teamWorked with digital banking SMEs, the project and product team, and business leaders to understand the new platform and what each audience needed to know.
  2. Mapped the client journeysBuilt each demo around one client task from start to finish, such as opening an account online, so teams could follow exactly what a client would see. I focused on the tasks clients would do most often and the ones they were most likely to call or visit a branch about.
  3. Built the demosScreen-captured the new app and website in the test environment, edited in Camtasia and finished in Premiere Pro. When screens changed, I re-recorded so every demo matched the live experience.
  4. LaunchedPosted the demos to SharePoint for on-demand use and featured them in launch training.

The Solution:

A set of guided demos that show the new app and website step by step, so team members can see exactly what clients will see.

  • Client's-eye view: Demos follow the real tasks clients complete, on both the app and the website.
  • Change readiness: Prepared teams ahead of launch so they could support clients from day one.
  • Consistent format: Every demo followed the same structure, so learners always knew what to expect as the rollout added new features.
  • Well received: The demos earned positive feedback from teams and leaders during the rollout.

Project 07 · CIBC US

PP&D Procedures Hub

My role
Experience designer, site architect and automation builder
Audience
Employees and site owners across five lines of business
Format
SharePoint hub, taxonomy, training and automated workflows
Tools
SharePoint, Power Automate, managed metadata

The Problem:

Procedures and job aids for private, personal and digital banking were spread across separate sites with different structures. Every document is tied to internal controls, so people need to find the current version quickly, and owners need to know when a document is due for review.

My Process:

  1. Aligned with stakeholdersWorked with a project manager and business leaders to plan one main site, with a sub site for each line of business owned by that business.
  2. Designed the structureBuilt a taxonomy using managed metadata and choice columns, so every document is tagged the same way across all five lines of business.
  3. Designed the experienceApplied UX principles to a homepage with tiered navigation: a button for each line of business plus shared library buttons.
  4. Automated and trainedBuilt Power Automate flows for review reminders and request tracking, and created training for the new site owners.

The Solution:

One central home for procedures and job aids, organized the way people look for them, with automation that keeps content current.

  • Findability: Tiered navigation and consistent metadata help people reach the right document in a few clicks.
  • Governance built in: A version-change flow reminds owners to update metadata, so content stays accurate.
  • Request intake: A request form and tracker with assignment alerts and weekly summaries keep updates moving.
  • Enablement: Site owner training gives each business the skills to manage its own content.
